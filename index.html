AISC ROBO RESCUE

Story: Martians kidnapped your robot pet! Cross 3 rooms and zap the Martian boss to rescue it. It takes about 1 minute.

Move: D-pad, arrow keys or WASD. Zap: A or B button, or Space (Room 3). Start: pause. Select: sound.

Room 1: grab the key, then reach the door. Room 2: find the pet tracker, then reach the door. Room 3: dodge the pink blobs and zap the Martian 5 times.

Touching an alien costs 1 of your 3 hearts. Win to get your PNG event ticket. Event: 26 Nov 2026, 5:30–7:30 PM, AISC, SJCE Mysuru.
